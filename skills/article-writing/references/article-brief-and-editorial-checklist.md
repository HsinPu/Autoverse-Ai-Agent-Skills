Use `fact`, `quotation`, `attributed opinion`, `analysis`, or `recommendation` for Type. Mark unsupported claims before drafting around them.

## Editorial passes

### Factual pass

- Verify names, dates, numbers, definitions, links, quotations, and comparisons.
- Match certainty to the source and surface material disagreement.
- Check that examples are real, representative, and not presented as broader proof.

### Structural pass

- Confirm the opening establishes the reader problem and the article's promise.
- Give each section one job and a clear connection to the controlling idea.
- Remove repeated setup, redundant summaries, and conclusions unsupported by the body.

### Line pass

- Prefer concrete nouns and verbs.
- Vary rhythm without sacrificing clarity.
- Replace vague intensifiers and promotional claims with evidence.
- Keep terminology, person, tense, capitalization, and formatting consistent.

### Publication-readiness pass

- Ensure the title and description match the delivered value.
- Confirm links and citations resolve to the intended evidence.
- Check accessibility of headings, tables, images, and link text.
- Record unresolved claims, approvals, rights, and publication actions separately.
