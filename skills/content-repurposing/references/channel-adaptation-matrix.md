## Quality gate

- Confirm factual fidelity and attribution.
- Confirm channel-native structure without unsupported sensationalism.
- Confirm brand voice, audience fit, CTA, and accessibility.
- Confirm image, music, footage, quotation, and likeness rights.
- Confirm time-sensitive platform constraints from a current authoritative source.
- Confirm publication remains a separate authorized action.
