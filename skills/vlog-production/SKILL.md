# Vlog Production

Use this skill when creating or improving vlog-style video content, from idea and story arc through filming plan, edit structure, captions, and platform packaging.

## Core Scope

- Personal vlog, travel vlog, founder vlog, lifestyle vlog, product journey, behind-the-scenes, day-in-the-life, and recap videos
- Concept, audience promise, emotional arc, talking-head beats, B-roll, and scene order
- Filming checklist, shot list, camera notes, natural-light notes, and continuity
- Footage organization, review, selects, story assembly, editing rhythm, pacing, jump cuts, music, captions, overlays, title, thumbnail, and repurposing
- Long-form YouTube, Shorts, Reels, TikTok, Douyin, Xiaohongshu, and LinkedIn video packaging

## Workflow

1. Clarify platform, target viewer, creator persona, goal, runtime, language, and available footage or filming constraints.
2. Define the vlog promise: why this moment matters, what changes, and why the viewer should stay.
3. Build a simple story arc: hook, context, journey, tension, payoff, reflection, and soft CTA.
4. Separate talking-head beats from B-roll, ambience, inserts, screen recordings, product shots, or location shots.
5. Create a filming or editing plan with must-have shots, nice-to-have shots, audio notes, and continuity notes.
6. If footage already exists, organize it before editing: copy originals, group clips, label dates/scenes, separate A-roll, B-roll, audio, screen recordings, photos, and exports.
7. Review the footage and make selects: mark strong moments, usable dialogue, reactions, transitions, mistakes worth keeping, missing context, bad audio, and unusable shots.
8. Build the edit story: arrange hook, context, sequence, tension, payoff, reflection, and CTA using the best available footage rather than raw chronology.
9. Shape the edit rhythm: opening 3 seconds, pacing, cutaways, music moments, caption beats, and retention resets.
10. Package for platform: title, thumbnail idea, description, hashtags, end card, and repurposed short clips.

## Vlog Story Rules

- A vlog is not just chronology; it needs a viewer-facing reason to watch.
- Keep the hook specific: a question, conflict, transformation, reveal, constraint, or emotional promise.
- Use voiceover or talking head to connect moments that raw footage does not explain.
- Use B-roll to prove the story, not decorate it randomly.
- Keep personal details relevant to the arc and viewer payoff.
- Prefer authentic, sensory, concrete moments over generic motivation or filler narration.

## Shot Planning

Capture or request:

- Establishing shots: location, time, weather, workspace, travel, event, or product context.
- Process shots: hands, walking, packing, setup, behind-the-scenes, screen, tools, mistakes, retries.
- Reaction shots: face, pause, laugh, surprise, frustration, relief, reflection.
- Detail shots: food, objects, signage, product, texture, notes, tickets, dashboards, props.
- Transition shots: doors, streets, transport, time-lapse, match cuts, screen transitions.
- Closing shot: lesson, result, after-state, next step, or quiet ending.

## Editing Checklist

- Preserve source files and edit from copies or project-managed media.
- Organize footage by date, scene, camera, audio, and role before cutting.
- Watch or skim all footage enough to know what story material exists.
- Create selects before the rough cut; do not start polishing while the story is still unstable.
- Build a rough cut that proves the story arc before adding detailed captions, music, color, or effects.
- Cut filler, repeated setup, dead air, weak transitions, and over-explained moments.
- Add captions for key lines, not every word when the style does not require full subtitles.
- Use music to support mood and pacing; do not let it fight the voice.
- Add retention resets every 10-30 seconds for short-form or dense social edits.
- Keep platform aspect ratio, safe zones, duration, and caption placement explicit.
- Export alternate hooks and thumbnails when the piece depends on discovery.

## Footage Review Log

When editing from existing material, create a lightweight review log:

```text
Clip | In | Out | Duration | Content | Use | Quality | Story Value | Notes
```

Use categories such as:

- `A-roll`: talking head, narration, interview, direct-to-camera.
- `B-roll`: location, action, product, travel, process, ambience.
- `Transition`: movement, doors, streets, time-lapse, screen changes.
- `Reaction`: emotion, mistake, surprise, reflection, natural pause.
- `Maybe`: imperfect but potentially useful for context or pacing.
- `Reject`: unusable audio, duplicate, shaky, irrelevant, accidental, unsafe.

## Footage Sampling Rules

Do not extract a fixed number of moments from every source file. Sample by duration and story density.

- `0-30s`: watch fully; mark 0-2 usable moments.
- `30s-3m`: skim once, then mark 1-5 usable moments.
- `3-10m`: skim at 2x/4x; mark 3-10 candidate moments.
- `10m+`: split into story sections first; mark 1-3 moments per section, capped at 10-20 candidates for the first pass.

Use two passes:

1. Candidate pass: collect moments with story, emotion, context, transition value, visual quality, or useful sound.
2. Selects pass: reduce candidates to moments that serve the final story arc.

For every 1 minute of finished vlog, expect roughly:

- 1-3 A-roll moments
- 4-10 B-roll moments
- 1-3 transition or reaction moments

## Clip Logging Rules

Every candidate or selected moment should record exact time ranges and a plain-language description.

- `Clip`: source file name.
- `In`: usable moment start timecode.
- `Out`: usable moment end timecode.
- `Duration`: selected moment length.
- `Content`: what happens in the moment.
- `Use`: A-roll, B-roll, Transition, Reaction, Hook, Payoff, Context, Maybe, or Reject.
- `Quality`: focus, stability, lighting, framing, audio, and any technical issue.
- `Story Value`: why the moment deserves to be in the edit.
- `Notes`: edit note, caption idea, music cue, volume fix, crop, or continuity note.

Example:

```text
Clip | In | Out | Duration | Content | Use | Quality | Story Value | Notes
IMG_0430.MOV | 00:03:45 | 00:04:08 | 23s | Says this is the first client-site shoot and laughs nervously | A-roll / Reaction | Audio low, image usable | Adds honesty before the main event | Raise volume, add captions
IMG_0451.MOV | 00:00:18 | 00:00:31 | 13s | Slow pan across workspace, lights, and product table | B-roll / Establishing | Stable, good light | Establishes location after hook | Use with light music
IMG_0470.MOV | 00:02:02 | 00:02:07 | 5s | Almost drops the product while picking it up | Reaction / Humor | Slight shake, still readable | Retention reset and human moment | Quick cut, maybe sound effect
```

## Reject Rules

Do not mark a moment as usable unless it has both technical usability and story value.

Reject or downgrade footage when:

- The image is blurry, out of focus, heavily smeared, or too shaky to understand.
- The camera is mostly pointed at the floor, ceiling, wall, bag, pocket, or accidental empty space.
- The subject is cut off in a way that makes the moment confusing or unusable.
- The audio is unintelligible and the visual does not work without it.
- The shot is a duplicate with no better expression, action, framing, or context.
- The moment has no story purpose, emotional value, transition value, or visual information.
- The clip exposes private, unsafe, copyrighted, or sensitive material that should not appear.
- The clip shows personal data, addresses, license plates, ID cards, account details, private messages, passwords, API keys, payment information, medical details, children, bystanders, or anyone who did not consent to appearing.
- The clip shows a computer or phone screen with sensitive tabs, notifications, customer data, internal dashboards, source code secrets, private documents, or unreleased business information.

Exceptions are allowed only when the flaw itself has story value, such as a funny mistake, chaos moment, or intentional transition. Mark those as `Maybe` first, not final selects.

Privacy and safety exceptions require explicit user approval and redaction planning before use.

## Output Shapes

Choose the output that fits the request:

- Vlog concept brief
- Talking-head script
- Filming checklist
- Shot list
- Edit decision list
- Footage review log
- B-roll request list
- Long-form outline plus short-form cutdowns
- Title/thumbnail/description package
